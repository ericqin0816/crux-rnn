# Figure captions and reading guide

All figures are original and generated by `cruxrnn figures --config
configs/v2/main.json` from saved, verified v2 data. Each link opens a PNG;
the adjacent SVG has the same basename. Colors identify blocked practice
(blue) and shuffled interleaved practice (orange) unless a legend specifies
architecture, capacity or schedule. A percentage-point difference is
interleaved minus blocked. No individual action prediction is an independent
statistical unit. Lines connect measured conditions and do not establish
intermediate effects. See the [report](../reports/v2_research_report.md) for
corrected tests, secondary outcomes and limitations.

1. [Experimental design](../results/v2/figures/experimental_design.png).
   One independent sequence family per run ID and distance, pretrained once
   per model variant and cloned into paired schedules. Initial weights, noisy
   example IDs and budgets match. Fresh evaluation precedes identical training
   on D. The diagram describes the protocol, rather than additional data.
2. [Recurrent architecture](../results/v2/figures/model_architecture.png).
   The complete seven-feature cue initializes seven-action decoding. Recurrent
   state, four raw readout logits and learned 4-to-7 feedback repeat across
   outputs; state resets between examples. RNN, GRU and LSTM share this outer
   interface. The implementation retains the legacy final unused transition.
3. [Practice schedules](../results/v2/figures/practice_schedules.png).
   Saved schedule orders from the first five-schedule main cell. Each column
   is one SGD minibatch; its color combines its actual A/B/C proportions.
   All five schedules contain the same complete permutation of example IDs.
   Trial cycling and random mixing are different manipulations.
4. [Sequence similarity](../results/v2/figures/sequence_similarity.png).
   Actual run-0 A/B/C bases for each k. Numbers encode action classes 0–3;
   each of the three pairwise Hamming distances equals k. At k=7 no shared
   position exists, so shared-position accuracy is undefined, never zero.
5. [Main accuracy](../results/v2/figures/main_accuracy.png).
   RNN h=7 final action accuracy, 100 independent pairs at every k=1–7.
   Panels show fresh-noisy and clean cues. Points and error bars are means
   ± one standard error across run IDs, not paired confidence intervals.
6. [Learning curves](../results/v2/figures/learning_curves.png).
   Main-training accuracy on fixed A/B/C development probes, 100 paired IDs
   per original distance. Rows show noisy and clean probes. Bands are ± one
   standard error. These development probes are separate from the primary
   final fresh-noise endpoint and are not extra independent samples.
7. [Paired effects](../results/v2/figures/paired_effects.png).
   All 100 seed-level fresh-noisy differences at each distance; horizontal
   offsets separate overlapping points and have no scientific meaning.
   Highlighted points are paired means; bars are pointwise 95% percentile
   bootstrap intervals. They are not simultaneous intervals. The dashed line
   denotes no schedule difference; the k=1 median is zero despite its positive
   mean. Effect sizes and Holm-adjusted tests are reported separately.
8. [Architecture comparison](../results/v2/figures/architecture_comparison.png).
   Schedule differences for five variants on the same 20 IDs at k=1,2,4,7.
   Legends give actual trainable parameter counts. GRU/LSTM h=3 approximate
   the RNN h=7 parameter count; they are not exact matches. Bars are pointwise
   paired bootstrap 95% intervals. Fixed budgets do not rank optimized models.
9. [Capacity sensitivity](../results/v2/figures/capacity_sensitivity.png).
   RNN h=7,16,32,64 on the same 20-ID subset. Bars are pointwise paired
   bootstrap 95% intervals. Larger capacity increased the gap at k=7 under
   this protocol; h=7 here must not be confused with the full 100-ID estimate.
10. [Practice granularity](../results/v2/figures/practice_granularity.png).
    Fresh-noisy means across the matched 20 IDs, equal exposure and update
    budgets. Curves compare five measured schedules. The figure displays
    means only; paired intervals and corrected contrasts are in the report.
    Large blocks at k=1 have a slightly negative, inconclusive difference.
11. [Forgetting and interference](../results/v2/figures/forgetting_interference.png).
    Mean old-sequence A/B/C noisy development accuracy during 100 D updates,
    100 paired IDs per original distance. No uncertainty bands are drawn.
    Pre-D levels differ, so raw loss and final retained accuracy answer
    different questions. Seed-level changes are retained in
    `results/v2/forgetting_distributions.csv`; IP's raw mean loss is greater
    at every k in the final endpoint analysis.
12. [Noise robustness](../results/v2/figures/noise_robustness.png).
    RNN h=7 final accuracy on fresh evaluation sets at cue SD=0,.1,.3,.6,1,
    100 pairs per original distance; training noise stays at SD=.1. Bars are
    means ± one standard error. The positive raw mean differences are
    descriptive, without an additional multiplicity-adjusted significance claim.
