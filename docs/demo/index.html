03 / Test the boundaries

Does the architecture matter?

Exploratory comparisons

Gates change both the computation and the number of trainable parameters. Equal hidden-state size does not mean equal capacity.

The comparison shows recorded means at the selected distance and hidden size. Read sample sizes with each estimate, and consult the report for parameter counts and matched comparisons.

A difference between architectures under one fixed budget does not identify which architecture is universally better.

04 / Interpret with care

A useful model has clear limits.

What the experiment can establish

How practice order changes cue decoding, sequence production and subsequent interference in these networks, under the recorded generator and training budget.

Action accuracy and exact-sequence success can disagree. Forgetting must be read alongside performance before learning D.

What it cannot establish

A mechanism in the human brain, biological consolidation, or an improvement in climbing performance. Climbing is a motivating analogy, not a measured outcome.

Previous exploratory outcomes were observed. Historical v1 results remain separate from the v2 experiment; missing or incomplete conditions are not filled in.
